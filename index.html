Welcome to Abby and Rob's Betton Brewhouse!

As friends and family, we welcome you into our home and invite you to explore and select a beer to enjoy with us.

Nestled in the heart of Tallahassee's Betton Hills neighborhood, Betton Brewhouse is Rob's personal passion project and homebrew hobby. What began as a curiosity in 2022 has grown into a true passion for the craft. Every batch is a new opportunity to learn, experiment, and improve.

Styles Brewed

  • 5 SRM Czech Pilsner

    A clean and crisp golden lager full of flavor with nicely balanced bitterness.

    • ABV: 5.3%
    • Fermentation: 4 weeks (cold lagered)
    • Hops: Saaz
    • Grains: Pilsner
    • IBU: 36

  • 6 SRM Golden Ale

    Crushable dry ale with biscuity notes and noticeable bitterness that pours a clear gold color ("Cold Gold").

    • ABV: 4.3%
    • Fermentation: 6 days
    • Hops: East Kent Goldings, Northern Brewer
    • Grains: Pale and Biscuit Malts
    • IBU: 44

  • 17 SRM Brown Ale

    A dry brown session ale with mild nut and chocolate flavors fit for drinking year-round and glass after glass.

    • ABV: 5.0%
    • Fermentation: 6 days
    • Hops: Northern Brewer, Cascade
    • Grains: Pale Malt, Caramel 40L, Chocolate Malt
    • IBU: 29

  • 7 SRM IPA

    A solid dry session ale with big citrus hop character and a slight haze.

    • ABV: 4.5%
    • Fermentation: 6 days
    • Hops: Centennial, Cascade, Citra
    • Grains: Pale Malt, Crystal 60, Flaked Oats
    • IBU: 43

  • 13 SRM Red Ale

    A work in-progress that's distinctly red and drinks like a lager.

    • ABV: 4.3%
    • Fermentation: 6 days
    • Hops: East Kent Goldings
    • Grains: Pale Malt, Caramel 40L, Roasted Barley
    • IBU: 26
